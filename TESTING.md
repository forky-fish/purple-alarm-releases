# Testing the beta

Thank you for testing. The most valuable thing you can do: **find out whether alarms ring when they
must.** Use a spare day, keep the phone's usual settings (do not exempt the test from your normal
battery saver), and note what happens.

## Must-do checks

| # | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create a one-time alarm two minutes ahead; swipe the app away; lock the phone | Screen lights, the ringing screen shows, sound and vibration run |
| 2 | Stop it from the ringing screen, then repeat and use *Schlummern* | Stop ends it at once; snooze rings again after the snooze time |
| 3 | Start a timer of one minute (*Timer & Stoppuhr*, reachable from the Wecker tab); swipe the app away | It rings when the time is up |
| 4 | Set an alarm for the next morning, restart the phone in the evening | It rings; no need to open the app |
| 5 | Leave the phone idle overnight with an alarm set | It rings on time |
| 6 | Pause a group for tomorrow, then look at the Calendar | The alarm is silent that day and the Calendar says why |

## Worth a look

- Public holidays: *Optionen → Feiertage*, add your country; an alarm with *An Feiertagen still* stays quiet.
- Shift plan: *Gruppen* → group → *Regeln und Schichtplan*.
- Backup: *Optionen → Gerät → Import und Export*.
- Anything that feels confusing, slow or ugly; texts that read badly.

## Reporting

Open an [issue](../../issues) with:

1. What you did and what you expected.
2. What happened instead, and the time if an alarm was missed.
3. Phone model, Android version, app version (*Optionen → Gerät → Erweitert → Über → Version*).
4. If the app offers it: *Optionen → Gerät → Fehlerprotokoll*, copy the text. It stays on your device until you copy it; check it for personal data before you paste it.

A missed alarm is always worth a report, even if you are not sure it was the app. Do not post your
alarm labels or holidays you consider private.
